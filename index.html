FAQ

Emergency Dentist, Same Day Dental Appointment & Toothache Relief: Your Questions Answered

Quick answers to the questions we hear most from patients in pain.

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe or lasting toothache, a knocked-out or cracked tooth, facial or gum swelling, a lost crown or filling, and bleeding that will not stop are all reasons to call an emergency dentist. If you have trouble breathing or swallowing, or a serious injury to the head or face, go to the nearest emergency room or call 911.

Can I get a same day dental appointment?

Prosperity Dental Group of West Palm Beach works to offer same day dental appointments for urgent problems. Availability depends on the day, so call 561-848-0087 as early as you can and tell us your symptoms so we can find the soonest opening.

What can I do for toothache relief until my visit?

Rinse your mouth with warm water, gently floss to remove trapped food, and use a cold compress on the outside of your cheek. An over-the-counter pain reliever taken as directed on the label may help. Avoid placing aspirin directly on the gum. These steps are temporary, so please have the cause checked by a dentist.

What should I do if my tooth is knocked out?

Call us right away. Pick the tooth up by the crown, not the root, and rinse it gently if it is dirty. If you can, place it back in the socket, or keep it in milk, and come in immediately. Acting within the first hour gives the tooth the best chance of being saved.

Do you treat children and adults for dental emergencies?

Yes. As a family dentist, Prosperity Dental Group of West Palm Beach cares for patients of different ages. Call us with your situation and we will let you know how we can help and what to bring to your visit.
